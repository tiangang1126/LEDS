               基于知识交互的人机协同作战评估方法

                                     牛莅原1　马 力1　朱晓敏1,*　游光荣1

                                                          （1. 军事科学院 战略评估咨询中心，北京　100091）

摘　要　作战评估强调评估作战任务进展，支持指挥员作战中的决策。智能化战争对现有作战评估方法提出了新挑战。在现
有人工智能方法的基础上，研究人机协同模式，提出人机紧密配合的作战评估方法。构建人机协同作战评估流程，设计人机
协同中知识交互效率和知识交互结果的验证实验。结合“观察罗盘行动”的实施过程，证明人机协同作战评估方法的效果，为
作战评估方法发展提供了新思路。
关键词　作战评估，人机协同，知识交互，机器学习

     Human-machine Collaboration Operation Assessment Based on Knowledge Exchange

                                              NIU Liyuan1　MA Li1　ZHU Xiaomin1,*　YOU Guangrong1

                                      （1. Consulting Center for Strategic Assessment, Academy of Military Sciences, Beijing 100091, China）

Abstract　Operation assessment emphasizes evaluating the progress of operation and supporting commanders' decision-making. Intelligent
warfare brings new challenges to existing operation assessment methods. Based on existing artificial intelligence methods, this study
examines human-machine collaboration modes and proposes an operation assessment method that involves close cooperation between
humans and machines. We constructed a process for operation assessment based on human-machine collaboration and designed
experiments to test the efficiency and results of knowledge exchange. Using data from the news about “Operation Observation Compass”, we
demonstrate the effectiveness of the operation assessment method based on human-machine collaboration, providing a new approach for the
development of operation assessment methods.
Key words　operation assessment, human-machine collaboration, knowledge exchange, machine learning
